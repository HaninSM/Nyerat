## Brand & Style

The design system embodies **Cyber-Cultural Neo-Javanesque**: an intersection of ancient royal Javanese calligraphy (Keraton elegance) and high-precision computer vision interaction. It shifts the perception of Hanacaraka from an intimidating, archaic script into a tactile, magical, and futuristic medium where the human hand shapes golden light in mid-air.

The visual atmosphere evokes a candlelit royal scriptorium reimagined through an augmented reality HUD. It is mysterious, dignified, high-tech, and reverent. The target audience spans Gen Z students discovering regional heritage, Muatan Lokal educators looking for gamified engagement, and typography enthusiasts. 

The aesthetic merges **Glassmorphism** with **Futuristic HUD Overlay Architecture**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep batik-charcoal dark mode layered with subtle geometric parang/kawung watermark grids.
- **Illumination:** Kinetic golden-bronze brushstrokes (`#E5A93C`, `#F3C766`) that leave particle ribbons in the air.
- **Telemetry & State:** Optical emerald/jade feedback rings (`#10B981`) validating hand tracking calibration, offset by deep terracotta pauses (`#C2410C`).
- **Tactile Kinesthetics:** Crisp glass panels hovering above real-time camera feeds, with glowing stroke vectors and precise circular countdown timers.

## Colors

The palette operates natively in dark mode to preserve contrast against glowing WebGL canvas strokes, low-light webcam feeds, and dynamic tracking HUDs.

- **Primary (`#E5A93C`) & Gold Light (`#F3C766`):** The royal Javanese bronze-gold (*prada emas*). Used for the active writing stroke trail, primary call-to-actions, accuracy achievement scores, and celebratory star emblems.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`) & Jade Glow (`#34D399`):** Symbolic of soothing jade (*giok*). Exclusively used for computer vision tracking lock, active webcam status, "Finger Detected" validation, and successful stroke pass states.
- **Tertiary (`#C2410C`) & Terracotta Glow (`#FB923C`):** Earthy Majapahit terracotta brick. Applied for system pauses, hand-lost alerts, countdown cancellations, and canvas reset warnings.
- **Neutral (`#121316`) & Charcoal Tiers (`#1A1C20`, `#24272E`):** Represents deep Javanese batik malam (wax resin) and carbon slate. Used for canvas backgrounds, HUD glass backdrops, and structured floating toolbars. Never use pure `#000000` to prevent harsh visual fatigue during webcam sessions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy blends three distinct narrative tones:

1. **Headlines (`Epilogue`):** Structural, sculptural, and expressive. Its chiselled geometric curves evoke stone temple inscriptions (*Prasasti*) while maintaining a high-fashion, contemporary stance.
2. **Body & Instructions (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Warm, humanist, and exceptionally legible. Developed with Indonesian cultural nuance, it ensures extended readability when rendering stroke guidance, philosophical backstories of Hanacaraka, and educational scorecards.
3. **HUD, Gestures & Telemetry (`Space Grotesk`):** Technical, monospaced-adjacent, and futuristic. Used for real-time tracking metrics (e.g., `FPS: 60`, `LANDMARK_8: ACTIVE`, `SIMILARITY: 94.2%`, `COUNTDOWN: 03s`). Uppercase styling with expanded tracking reinforces the computer vision interface.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a full-bleed, dynamic **Vision Workspace Canvas** backed by a 12-column adaptive fluid grid for desktop and a 4-column HUD stack for compact viewports.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** The primary view hosts the full-aspect camera canvas at center-left (columns 1–8 or 1–9) with fixed golden ratio margins. The right side (columns 9–12 or 10–12) holds the Hanacaraka script reference card, stroke order playback, gesture state monitor, and score telemetry.
- **Tablet & Laptop (768px – 1023px):** A split 8-column layout where gesture status panels dock as floating glass pills along the bottom edge of the camera viewport.
- **Mobile Handheld (360px – 767px):** Canvas scales to a 4:3 or 16:9 upper viewport; controls collapse into bottom sheets and gesture guide floating chips.

Grid gutters strictly maintain `1.25rem` (`20px`) on mobile and `2rem` (`32px`) on desktop. Internal HUD containers adhere to an 8px modular spacing rhythm (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) to anchor floating coordinate indicators cleanly over dynamic video streams.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through translucent glass surfaces layered above the real-time webcam feed rather than traditional drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background `#121316` hosting the raw camera feed with an overlaid 5% radial vignette and an ultra-subtle golden grid coordinate matrix (`rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.05)`).
- **Level 1 (Docked HUD Panels):** Surface `#1A1C20` rendered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, `background: rgba(26, 28, 32, 0.75)`, and a hairline ghost border of `1px solid rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.15)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Status Pods & Script Guides):** Raised floating cards (`rgba(36, 39, 46, 0.85)`) framed by dual ambient rim lights:
  - Default: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
  - Active Gesture (Tracking): `0 0 24px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25), 0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`
  - Inking Trail (Drawing): Outer stroke glow `0 0 20px rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.45)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Scoring & Certificate Delivery):** High-priority backdrop with `rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.88)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, and golden-bronze ambient glow `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design uses **Rounded (Value 2)** geometries. Standard cards, HUD panels, and webcam viewports feature a `0.5rem` (`8px`) radius, expanding to `1rem` (`16px`) for primary cards and modal dialogs. 

To bridge computer vision telemetry with organic calligraphy:
- **Gesture HUD Badges & Action Chips:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`) to match fluid hand movements.
- **Stroke Guidance Overlays:** Rounded terminal caps (`round` line join/cap) on target vectors to mimic traditional reed pen and Chinese-inked bamboo nibs (*kropak*).
- **Camera Viewfinder Brackets:** Distinctive corner-bracket cutouts framing the hand-interaction zone, blending soft radii with precision reticles.

## Components

### 1. Kinesthetic Action Buttons (Interactive & Gesture Triggered)
- **Primary (Golden Prada):** Solid `#E5A93C` with text `#121316` (Bold `Space Grotesk`). Hover state shifts to `#F3C766` with a `0 0 20px rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.4)` glow.
- **Secondary (Glass Slate):** Translucent `#1A1C20` with `1px solid rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.3)` and `#F3C766` text.
- **Air-Gesture Action Trigger:** Includes an integrated circular countdown ring (`SVG stroke-dashoffset`) that fills over 3 seconds when the user holds a confirmed pose (e.g., Thumbs Up 👍 for "Next Letter").

### 2. Hand Pose HUD Indicator Chips
- **States:**
  - *Drawing (☝️ Single Index):* Gold border (`#E5A93C`), gold dot pulsing at 1Hz, label `TINTA AKTIF`.
  - *Pause / Prep (✌️ Peace):* Terracotta border (`#C2410C`), circular 3-2-1 timer ring, label `JEDA SESI`.
  - *Next Letter (👍 Thumbs Up):* Jade border (`#10B981`), progressive progress bar, label `KUNCI & LANJUT`.
  - *Clear (🖐️ Open Palm):* Terracotta fill wash `rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.15)`, label `HAPUS KANVAS`.
- **Styling:** Pill container with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, height `36px`, typography `label-sm` in `Space Grotesk`.

### 3. Aksara Guidance & Evaluation Cards
- **Ghost Stroke Canvas:** Target Hanacaraka letter rendered in dashed golden guide paths with glowing, directional arrows indicating stroke order and curvature direction.
- **Live Vector Diff Canvas:** Real-time user stroke drawn on top in radiant `#FFD580` glowing brush line.
- **Score Appreciation Card:** High-elevation glass card featuring:
  - Big metric score (`88% Kemiripan`) in `Epilogue` headline font.
  - Fréchet distance feedback pill (e.g., *"Lekukan kedua kurang melengkung 15%"*).
  - 3-tier star rating rendered in faceted brass-gold icons.

### 4. Input Fields & Email Delivery Form
- **Form Surface:** Deep charcoal `#1A1C20` with `1px solid rgba(243, 199, 102, 0.2)` border. Focused state transitions to `1px solid #E5A93C` with an ambient inner shadow.
- **Typography:** `body-md` in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with placeholder in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`.
- **Submit Action:** "Kirim Font Aksara Jawa (.TTF)" with tactile click feedback and instant jade validation toast upon successful dispatch.

### 5. Checkboxes, Toggles & Radio Options
- **Aksara Level Selector (Nglegena vs Sandhangan):** Segmented glass pill control. Active segment is filled with solid `#E5A93C` and dark text `#121316`.
- **Toggle Switches (Webcam Guide & Sound FX):** Compact 20px track in `#24272E`. When switched on, track illuminates with emerald jade `#10B981` and thumb shifts with a swift spring transition.
